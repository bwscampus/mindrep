# Narration script: Lesson 2.2, "Effort Over Talent"

- **Length:** about 2 minutes with pauses (about 200 words). With the activity and quiz, the
  whole lesson stays around 5 minutes.
- **File:** `public/audio/lessons/2.2-effort-over-talent.mp3`
- **Voice:** warm but firm. The listener might be the gifted kid or the kid who got cut, so
  don't talk down to either one.

Recording steps and voice settings are in [`README.md`](README.md). Paste only the
**Paste into ElevenLabs** blocks. The *italic* lines are delivery cues for you.

---

## Part 1: Two kids (Hook)

*Storytelling. A little nostalgic, like everyone knows this kid.*

> Every team has one.
>
> The kid who's naturally faster, stronger, more skilled than everyone else.
>
> By sophomore year, coaches were calling him a future Division One star.

`[pause 1.5s]`

*Drop the energy on the turn.*

> By senior year… he was off the team.
>
> Not because he lost his talent.
>
> Because he stopped working.

`[pause 2s]`

*Lift. This is the hopeful half.*

> Meanwhile, the kid who got cut from JV twice made varsity. On effort alone.

`[pause 1.5s]`

> Talent without work is just potential.
>
> Effort is what turns potential… into results.

`[pause 2s]`

*Gentle and curious.*

> Has someone with less talent than you ever outworked you? Think about how that felt.

`[pause 3s]`

---

## Part 2: The rare thing (Lesson)

*Lean in. This is the truth nobody tells you.*

> Here's the truth about talent that almost nobody tells you.
>
> Talent is common.
>
> Maximum effort is rare.

`[pause 2s]`

> In Trevor Moawad's work with elite teams, the athletes who lasted longest weren't always the most gifted.
>
> They were the ones who stayed fifteen extra minutes after practice.
>
> Who showed up on the days they didn't feel like it.
>
> Who competed in practice exactly the way they competed in games.

`[pause 2s]`

*Slow and certain. Land on "every time".*

> Effort is the one part of your performance you control every single time.
>
> Not some of the time. Every time.

`[pause 2s]`

---

## Part 3: Bridge to the activity

> Next, you'll rate your effort this week, honestly, from zero to five.
>
> Nobody's grading you. It's just where you're starting from.

`[end: about 1.5s of silence, then fade out]`
