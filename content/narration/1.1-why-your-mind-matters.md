# Narration script: Lesson 1.1, "Why Your Mind Matters"

A draft to hear and refine, not final copy. It covers the Hook and Lesson screens, which is
what the read-along shows. The activity and quiz stay interactive after it.

- **Length:** about 2 minutes with pauses (about 245 words). With the activity and quiz, the
  whole lesson stays around 5 minutes.
- **File:** `public/audio/lessons/1.1-why-your-mind-matters.mp3`
- **Voice:** warm and unhurried, like talking to one athlete in the locker room, not
  performing.

## How to use this

1. Paste only the text in the **Paste into ElevenLabs** block.
2. Starting settings, to adjust by ear: stability about 35%, similarity about 75–80%, style
   low. Generate 4–6 takes of each part and keep the best one.
3. Before choosing, try 2–3 voices on Part 1 only, and let a couple of peers pick.
4. In Audacity, put the takes together and add the pauses marked `[pause Ns]`. A generated
   pause is usually too short, so real silence works better. Add a 1s fade-in, a 2s fade-out
   and about 1.5s of silence at each end.
5. Export as MP3, mono, 64–96 kbps. If there's an ambient bed, keep it about 18–24 dB under
   the voice.

Delivery cues in *italics* are for you, not the voice tool. Commas slow the voice down, an
ellipsis (…) adds a hesitation, and an em dash (—) adds a sharp break.

## Part 1: The moment (Hook)

*Quiet and close. Start slowly and let the picture form.*

> **Paste into ElevenLabs**
>
> Final seconds. Score's tied.
>
> The ball is in your hands… and everyone in the building knows it.
>
> Two athletes could be standing right where you are.
>
> The first one is thinking, what if I miss. Everyone's watching. I always blow the big moments.
>
> The second one is thinking… this is what I train for. One shot. Focus.
>
> Same game. Same pressure. Same shot.
>
> Different minds — different results.

`[pause 2s]`

*A little warmer here. It's a real question, so leave room for them to answer it.*

> Which one sounds more like you, right now?
>
> Be honest. Nobody's listening but you.

`[pause 3s]`

---

## Part 2: The turn (Lesson)

*Lift slightly. This is good news, so say it like good news.*

> Here's what most athletes never get told.
>
> The way you think under pressure isn't something you're born with, or stuck with.
>
> It's a skill. You can train it, just like speed, just like strength, just like your first step.

`[pause 1.5s]`

*Steady, with respect. Not a hype voice.*

> Trevor Moawad spent his career in rooms with NFL quarterbacks, Olympians, and some of the most elite military teams in the world.
>
> And what he found, again and again, was simple.
>
> The mind leads… and the body follows.
>
> Your mind can be trained.

`[pause 2s]`

*Slower. Put the weight on the last words of each line.*

> Athletes who train their minds handle pressure better.
>
> They bounce back from mistakes faster.
>
> And when the big moment comes, they don't hope they're ready.
>
> They know.

`[pause 2s]`

---

## Part 3: The invitation (bridge to the activity)

*Warm, and quieter again. You're handing it back to them.*

> So that's what this is. Your mental training camp.
>
> A few minutes a day. No magic. Just reps.
>
> And like any training, it starts with knowing where you are today.
>
> In a moment, you're going to rate yourself. No right answers. No wrong ones.
>
> Just an honest starting line.

`[pause 1.5s]`

> Let's find yours.

`[end: about 1.5s of silence, then fade out]`

---

### Notes on the choices

- The on-screen line "Sports Psychology Research" is an unattributed quote, so it isn't read
  aloud. The spoken version stays with Moawad's idea instead.
- "Your first step" is sport-neutral on purpose. One file has to serve every sport.
- About 245 spoken words. At an unhurried ~140 wpm plus the marked pauses, that's roughly
  2 minutes. The scripts follow the screen text, so they aren't padded to a length.
