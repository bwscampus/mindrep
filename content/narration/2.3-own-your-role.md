# Narration script: Lesson 2.3, "Own Your Role"

- **Length:** about 2 minutes with pauses (about 200 words). With the activity and quiz, the
  whole lesson stays around 5 minutes.
- **File:** `public/audio/lessons/2.3-own-your-role.mp3`
- **Voice:** respectful and steady. For an athlete who feels overlooked, it should sound
  like being seen, not like a pep talk.

Recording steps and voice settings are in [`README.md`](README.md). Paste only the
**Paste into ElevenLabs** blocks. The *italic* lines are delivery cues for you.

---

## Part 1: The backup (Hook)

*Quiet and understated. The story is dramatic enough on its own.*

> In 2014, Malcolm Butler was an undrafted rookie cornerback.
>
> He barely made the team.

`[pause 1.5s]`

> Then came the Super Bowl. The game on the line.
>
> And he was the one on the field.
>
> He intercepted the pass that won the championship.

`[pause 2s]`

*Slower. This is the point.*

> He wasn't the starter. He wasn't the star.
>
> But he'd owned his role so completely that when his moment came… he was ready.

`[pause 1.5s]`

> Trevor Moawad's lesson: the athlete who masters their role, no matter how small, earns bigger ones.

`[pause 2s]`

*Gentle. It's an honest question.*

> Do you ever feel like your role is too small? Sit with that feeling for a second.

`[pause 3s]`

---

## Part 2: The roles no one wants (Lesson)

*Clear and even.*

> Every team has a star.
>
> But teams win because of the athletes who own the roles nobody else wants.
>
> Coming off the bench with energy. Being the defensive stopper. Setting the screen so someone else scores.

`[pause 2s]`

*Tell this like a story, with a small lift on "Michael Jordan passed him the ball".*

> Take Steve Kerr. He averaged about six points a game over his career.
>
> But in the 1997 NBA Finals, with the title on the line, Michael Jordan passed him the ball…
>
> and Kerr hit the shot that won the championship.

`[pause 2s]`

> Why? Because he knew his role. He prepared for it completely. And he played it without ego.

`[pause 1.5s]`

*Slow, with weight on the final word of each line.*

> Ego fights the role.
>
> Excellence masters it.

`[pause 2s]`

---

## Part 3: Bridge to the activity

> Next, tap the statements that describe your role right now.
>
> Not the role you want. The one you have… because that's where it starts.

`[end: about 1.5s of silence, then fade out]`

---

### Notes on the choices

- The Kerr facts match the corrected screen: about 6 ppg for his career (ESPN lists 6.0),
  and the title-clinching shot in the 1997 Finals.
- The hook doesn't name Super Bowl XLIX by number, because numerals are hard to say
  naturally. The screen still shows it.
