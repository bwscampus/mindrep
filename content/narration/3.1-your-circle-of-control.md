# Narration script: Lesson 3.1, "Your Circle of Control"

- **Length:** about 2 minutes with pauses (about 200 words). With the activity and quiz, the
  whole lesson stays around 5 minutes.
- **File:** `public/audio/lessons/3.1-your-circle-of-control.mp3`
- **Voice:** calm and clarifying. This lesson takes weight off the listener, so the
  delivery should feel like a relief.

Recording steps and voice settings are in [`README.md`](README.md). Paste only the
**Paste into ElevenLabs** blocks. The *italic* lines are delivery cues for you.

---

## Part 1: Marcus (Hook)

*Storytelling, a bit tense at the start. Marcus is frustrated.*

> Marcus was one of the best quarterbacks in his state. Recruited. Talented. Hungry.
>
> And for two years, he was furious at his offensive line.
>
> Missed blocks. Sacks. Every mistake they made, he carried it.

`[pause 1.5s]`

> His stats dropped. His frustration grew.

`[pause 2s]`

*Slow down. The question should land like a door opening.*

> Then a mental coach asked him one question.
>
> Which of those things can you actually control?

`[pause 2s]`

> The answer was… none of them.
>
> He'd been burning all that energy on problems he had no power over.

`[pause 2s]`

*Gentle. Turn it toward the listener.*

> What's something in your sport that takes up your energy… but isn't actually yours to control?

`[pause 3s]`

---

## Part 2: Two circles (Lesson)

*Clear and spacious. Picture two circles and let the lists breathe.*

> Trevor Moawad teaches athletes to split everything into two circles.
>
> Inside the first circle is everything you control. Your effort. Your attitude. Your preparation. Your focus. How you respond to mistakes.

`[pause 2s]`

> Outside it is everything you don't. The weather. The referees. Your opponents. Your coach's decisions. The crowd. How your teammates play.

`[pause 2s]`

*Warm and certain.*

> Athletes who put their energy inside the first circle handle pressure better.
>
> Because your focus only goes so far.
>
> Every rep you spend worrying about the outside circle… is a rep you don't get back.

`[pause 2s]`

> So spend it where it counts.
>
> Focus where you have power. Release where you don't.

`[pause 2s]`

---

## Part 3: Bridge to the activity

> Next, you'll sort some things into the two circles. In your control, or not.
>
> Some will be obvious. A few might surprise you.

`[end: about 1.5s of silence, then fade out]`

### Notes on the choices

- The count of years is "two", matching the corrected title.
- Marcus is an illustrative composite on the screen too, so the script doesn't present him
  as a named real person beyond what the screen does.
- The screen's "key insight from sports psychology" line is spoken without the label. The
  claim itself is kept general.
