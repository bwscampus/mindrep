# Narration script: Lesson 1.3, "The Reset Button"

- **Length:** about 2 minutes with pauses (about 200 words). With the activity and quiz, the
  whole lesson stays around 5 minutes.
- **File:** `public/audio/lessons/1.3-the-reset-button.mp3`
- **Voice:** steady and practical, like a coach teaching a drill. It's the most "how-to" of
  the Module 1 lessons.

Recording steps and voice settings are in [`README.md`](README.md). Paste only the
**Paste into ElevenLabs** blocks. The *italic* lines are delivery cues for you.

---

## Part 1: Three seconds (Hook)

*Lean in a little, like you're sharing something most people don't know.*

> The best athletes in the world have a hidden weapon they use after every mistake.
>
> It takes three seconds.
>
> It resets the brain. It stops the spiral. It puts them right back in the game.

`[pause 1.5s]`

> It's called a reset routine.
>
> And by the end of today… you'll have your own.

`[pause 2s]`

*Real question. Give them time to answer it honestly.*

> So think about it. Right after you make a mistake in a game… what do you actually do?

`[pause 3s]`

---

## Part 2: Recognize, Release, Refocus (Lesson)

*Clear and rhythmic. Say each step name slowly, then explain it.*

> Elite athletes use a three-step reset between plays. Three R's.
>
> One. Recognize. Notice the mistake. Take one breath. Don't judge it.

`[pause 1.5s]`

> Two. Release. Give your body a signal. Shake out your hands, take a deep breath, fix your gear. It tells your brain, that moment is done.

`[pause 1.5s]`

> Three. Refocus. Say your neutral phrase. Lock your eyes on the next play… and go.

`[pause 2s]`

*Slow down. This is the part that makes the routine work.*

> That physical cue is the key.
>
> Your body and your brain are connected. When your body lets go, it breaks the loop in your head.

`[pause 1.5s]`

> Quarterbacks do this between every play. Tennis players do it between every point.
>
> Now it's your turn.
>
> Recognize. Release. Refocus.

`[pause 2s]`

---

## Part 3: Bridge to the activity

*Warm, and a little proud of them.*

> Next, you're going to build your own reset, one choice from each step.
>
> Pick what actually feels like you. This is the one you'll use in real games.

`[end: about 1.5s of silence, then fade out]`

---

### Notes on the choices

- "NFL quarterbacks" on screen becomes "Quarterbacks" in the script. It's the same meaning
  and flows better when spoken.
- The step numbers are spoken ("One. Recognize.") so a listener who isn't looking at the
  screen can follow the order.
