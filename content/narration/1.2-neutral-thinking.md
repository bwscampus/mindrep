# Narration script: Lesson 1.2, "Neutral Thinking"

- **Length:** about 2 minutes with pauses (about 200 words). With the activity and quiz, the
  whole lesson stays around 5 minutes.
- **File:** `public/audio/lessons/1.2-neutral-thinking.mp3`
- **Voice:** the same voice as the other lessons. Calm and direct. This one is about not
  over-reacting, so the delivery shouldn't over-react either.

Recording steps and voice settings are in [`README.md`](README.md). Paste only the
**Paste into ElevenLabs** blocks. The *italic* lines are delivery cues for you.

---

## Part 1: The spiral (Hook)

*Start in the moment, a little tight, like the mistake just happened.*

> You miss. And before your feet even land, your brain has already said it.
>
> I'm terrible. Why does this always happen to me?
>
> That thought turns into tension. The tension turns into another mistake.
>
> And just like that… the spiral has started.

`[pause 2s]`

*Soften. Slow down. Offer the way out.*

> But what if you could stop it… before it starts?
>
> That's what neutral thinking does. And it's one of the most powerful tools the best athletes in the world use.

`[pause 1.5s]`

> Think about the last time a mistake sent you spiraling in a game.
>
> Hold that moment for a second.

`[pause 3s]`

---

## Part 2: Three voices (Lesson)

*Even and clear. Let each of the three voices sound different, but don't act them out.*

> Trevor Moawad's most powerful idea is simple. Neutral thinking.
>
> Here's how it works. Same miss, three different voices in your head.
>
> The negative voice says, I missed that shot. I'm the worst. This team deserves better than me.
>
> The neutral voice says, I missed that shot. The next one is all that matters.
>
> And the positive voice? It can help… but under real pressure, it can start to feel fake.

`[pause 2s]`

*Warmer. This is the heart of the lesson, so give it room.*

> Neutral works because it's true, and it moves you forward.
>
> You don't pretend the mistake didn't happen.
>
> You just don't let it write your story.

`[pause 2s]`

*Slower, with weight on the last word of each line.*

> The past is not predictive.
>
> The next moment is the only one you control.
>
> Facts. Accept. Next action. That's the neutral loop.

`[pause 2s]`

---

## Part 3: Bridge to the activity

*Lighter, handing it over.*

> Now it's your turn.
>
> You're about to see some thoughts that probably sound familiar. Tap each one, and flip it to neutral.
>
> No pretending. Just facts… and forward.

`[end: about 1.5s of silence, then fade out]`
