# Narration script: Lesson 2.1, "No Excuses, No Limits"

- **Length:** about 2 minutes with pauses (about 200 words). With the activity and quiz, the
  whole lesson stays around 5 minutes.
- **File:** `public/audio/lessons/2.1-no-excuses-no-limits.mp3`
- **Voice:** grounded and honest. It's a story about someone's own mistakes, so tell it
  without judgment and without making it dramatic.

Recording steps and voice settings are in [`README.md`](README.md). Paste only the
**Paste into ElevenLabs** blocks. The *italic* lines are delivery cues for you.

---

## Part 1: Kyle Long (Hook)

*Plain, like telling a true story to one person. Don't make it sound tragic.*

> Kyle Long had a scholarship to pitch at Florida State.
>
> He lost it. Poor grades… bad choices off the field.

`[pause 1.5s]`

> He could have blamed the school. The coaches. Bad luck.
>
> He didn't.

`[pause 1.5s]`

*Build slowly here. Each line is a step up.*

> He owned it.
>
> He went to a community college, switched to football, and rebuilt — one practice at a time.
>
> Then Oregon.
>
> And in 2013, he was a first-round NFL draft pick.

`[pause 2s]`

> The difference wasn't talent.
>
> It was a mindset Trevor Moawad calls, it takes what it takes.

`[pause 2s]`

*Softer. It's a question, not an accusation.*

> Be honest with yourself for a second. Have you ever used an excuse… the weather, your teammates, a coach… to explain a bad game?

`[pause 3s]`

---

## Part 2: Outsourcing (Lesson)

*Even and clear.*

> Moawad noticed a pattern in athletes who never reach their potential.
>
> They outsource their results.

`[pause 1.5s]`

> It sounds like, the ref was unfair. My teammates didn't show up. I didn't sleep well.
>
> And here's the thing… none of those are necessarily false.

`[pause 1.5s]`

*Slower. Put the weight on "control".*

> But the moment you give them power over how you play, you give away your control.

`[pause 2s]`

*Warm and certain. This is the takeaway.*

> It takes what it takes means this.
>
> Whatever the circumstances… you still choose your response.
>
> The conditions don't decide.
>
> You decide.

`[pause 2s]`

---

## Part 3: Bridge to the activity

> Next, you'll see some excuses you've probably heard. Maybe even said.
>
> Tap each one, and flip it into ownership.

`[end: about 1.5s of silence, then fade out]`

  10–18 audience.
