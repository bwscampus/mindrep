# Narration script: Lesson 3.2, "Let Go of the Scoreboard"

- **Length:** about 2 minutes with pauses (about 200 words). With the activity and quiz, the
  whole lesson stays around 5 minutes.
- **File:** `public/audio/lessons/3.2-let-go-of-the-scoreboard.mp3`
- **Voice:** hushed at the start, like a silent stadium, then opening up.

Recording steps and voice settings are in [`README.md`](README.md). Paste only the
**Paste into ElevenLabs** blocks. The *italic* lines are delivery cues for you.

---

## Part 1: Match point (Hook)

*Very quiet. Short lines. Let the silence carry the pressure.*

> Match point.
>
> Down two sets.
>
> The crowd has gone silent.

`[pause 2s]`

> She has one choice. Watch the scoreboard spiral… or play tennis.

`[pause 1.5s]`

*Almost a whisper, as if it's her own voice.*

> She tells herself, one ball. One point. That's all that exists.

`[pause 2s]`

*Slowly build energy, one line at a time.*

> She wins that point.
>
> Then the next.
>
> Then the set.
>
> Then the match.

`[pause 2s]`

> Nobody remembers the score after the first set.
>
> They remember who played their best at the moment it mattered most.
>
> This is called process focus.

`[pause 2s]`

> Have you ever gotten so locked on the score that you forgot to just… play?

`[pause 3s]`

---

## Part 2: Process over outcome (Lesson)

*Calm and explanatory.*

> Here's something elite coaches know.
>
> Athletes who fixate on the scoreboard often tighten up under pressure.

`[pause 1.5s]`

> Because the score is an outcome. And outcomes are outside your circle of control.

`[pause 2s]`

*Slow down for the list, and give each item a beat.*

> You don't control whether the ball goes in.
>
> You control your footwork. Your release. Your follow-through. Your mental state.

`[pause 2s]`

> Trevor Moawad trained athletes to let go of the scoreboard during the game.
>
> Their cue was simple. Play the next play perfectly.

`[pause 1.5s]`

*Warm and settled. This is the release.*

> Win the play in front of you.
>
> The scoreboard takes care of itself.

`[pause 2s]`

---

## Part 3: Bridge to the activity

> Next, you'll see some scoreboard thoughts. Flip each one into something you can actually do.
>
> One play at a time.

`[end: about 1.5s of silence, then fade out]`
