# Narration script: Lesson 3.3, "The 90-Second Rule"

- **Length:** about 2 minutes with pauses (about 200 words). With the activity and quiz, the
  whole lesson stays around 5 minutes.
- **File:** `public/audio/lessons/3.3-the-90-second-rule.mp3`
- **Voice:** the calmest of the nine. It's about letting a feeling pass, so the pacing
  should do that too.

Recording steps and voice settings are in [`README.md`](README.md). Paste only the
**Paste into ElevenLabs** blocks. The *italic* lines are delivery cues for you.

---

## Part 1: Ninety seconds (Hook)

*Curious and a little fascinated. This is a cool fact about your own body.*

> Neuroscientist Dr. Jill Bolte Taylor noticed something about how emotions work.
>
> When something stressful happens, your brain fires an emotional response. Stress chemicals flood your system.

`[pause 1.5s]`

*Slow. Let "about ninety seconds" land.*

> And that surge… lasts about ninety seconds.

`[pause 2s]`

> After that, the chemicals start to clear.
>
> Unless you keep the thought alive.

`[pause 1.5s]`

*A little heavier. Everyone recognizes this.*

> Every time you replay the mistake… complain about the call… relive the bad moment… you restart the clock.

`[pause 2s]`

> Elite athletes use this.
>
> Let the ninety seconds pass. Then move.

`[pause 2s]`

*Gentle and honest.*

> Think about your last tough game. After a mistake, how long did you stay upset? Thirty seconds? Five minutes? The whole game?

`[pause 3s]`

---

## Part 2: The reset protocol (Lesson)

*Clear and unhurried. Three stages, each with its own space.*

> Here's how you can use it.
>
> For the first ninety seconds, feel it. Don't push it down. Breathe. Let it clear.

`[pause 2s]`

> Then, at ninety seconds, ask yourself one question.
>
> What's the next controllable?

`[pause 2s]`

> After that, all your energy moves forward. That moment is done.

`[pause 2s]`

*Warm. This line matters most for younger athletes. It's permission, not suppression.*

> This isn't ignoring your feelings.
>
> It's respecting how your body works… and then choosing to move forward.

`[pause 2s]`

> Feel it for ninety seconds. Then choose your next move.

`[pause 2s]`

---

## Part 3: Bridge to the activity

> Next, you'll rate how fast you usually bounce back in a few tough situations.
>
> Zero means stuck all game. Five means back in ninety seconds. Wherever you are is fine.

`[end: about 1.5s of silence, then fade out]`

---

### Notes on the choices

- "About ninety seconds" is Taylor's own framing, and the screen now says the same.
- "Discovered something remarkable" became "noticed something about how emotions work",
  which is a more modest claim to say aloud.
- The unsourced "fewer mental errors" claim was removed from the screen, so it isn't here.
- Numbers are written as words ("ninety") so the voice reads them the same way every time.
